---
name: weekly-review
description: A short look back at the last seven days of the journal
when: the person asks how their week went, or for a weekly review
enabled: true
---
1. Read the rows of /journal.csv from the last 7 days.
2. Group them by day and say in a few lines what happened, in the person's own words.
3. Count the entries with collection_aggregate and say how many days have none.
4. Ask whether they want to add anything the journal is missing.
